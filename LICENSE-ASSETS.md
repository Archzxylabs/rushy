# Rushy asset scope

The 12 Meridian character source illustrations, five district illustrations, and 12 original ability VFX sheets in `assets/meridian/source-art/` were generated for this game with the built-in image generation tool. Runtime portraits, icons, cut-ins, atlases, 36 ability sprites, other effects, and optimized backgrounds were derived from these sources and from the Meridian Canvas rig/effect code. They are separate from the MIT source-code license.

The only reused media from the previous local game build are generic instrumental music, stage ambience, and generic round announcer cues in `assets/meridian/audio/`. Character-specific CHOG voice lines and CHOG artwork are absent. The Barlow font is distributed under its SIL Open Font License in `assets/meridian/fonts/barlow-OFL.txt`.

This game has no official association with Arc or Circle and uses no Arc or Circle marks in its artwork.
